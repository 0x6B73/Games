Gold Bars unlock equipment and favors that ordinary cash cannot buy. They can appear as an exceptionally rare Ask for Help reward, or you can exchange cash at an intentionally brutal rate.

PROPERTIES
HOW PROPERTIES WORK ?
Properties are the legitimate-looking backbone of your organization. They generate automatic hourly income even while you are not running Jobs, help offset equipment upkeep, and turn one-time Job money into a permanent revenue stream. Each additional copy costs more, so building a property empire is a long-term investment.
FACILITIES 0 / 4 built
HOW FACILITIES WORK ?
Facilities are permanent organization infrastructure. They are expensive, one-time purchases that unlock automation systems and provide modest business income. Facilities survive Kingpin Prestige.
