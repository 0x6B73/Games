Properties

WHY BUY PROPERTIES?

Properties are the legitimate-looking backbone of your organization. They generate automatic hourly income even while you are not running Jobs, help offset equipment upkeep, and turn one-time Job money into a permanent revenue stream. Each additional copy costs more, so building a property empire is a long-term investment.
